# Sources

Research behind the catalog was compiled in October 2026. The catalog and scanner are written independently, in this skill's own words and code. These sources informed the pattern list and remedies.

- Anthropic, "Improving frontend design through Skills": coins the distributional-convergence framing and calls out Inter, purple gradients and minimal motion. https://claude.com/blog/improving-frontend-design-through-skills
- funboy322/avoid-ai-design: a tell catalog with P0/P1/P2 severity, scanner/render/judgment tiers, and second-order defaults. https://github.com/funboy322/avoid-ai-design
- 925 Studios, "AI Slop Web Design: Complete Guide": uniform spacing and radius, copy specificity, motion with a job, and real imagery. https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-web-design-guide
- 925 Studios, "AI Slop Fonts and Gradients": the four-question audit and the Tailwind indigo lineage. https://www.925studios.co/blog/ai-slop-design-tells
- The Fountain Institute, "7 Signs a UI Has Been Vibe Coded": neon, glow, emoji icons, nested cards, status dots, and the 60/30/10 rule. https://www.thefountaininstitute.com/blog/signs-vibe-coded-ui
- "Hallmark: Stop AI-Generated UI Slop": the default section waterfall and the token-drift framing. https://dev.to/rams901/hallmark-stop-ai-generated-ui-slop-in-one-command-in-2026-3p9n
- Superdesign, "How to Make AI UI Look Less Generic": a DESIGN.md as the source of truth, and planning direction before code. https://superdesign.dev/blog/how-to-make-ai-ui-look-less-generic
- Superdesign, "How to Design a SaaS Dashboard UI With AI": missing empty and loading states, and inconsistent inputs across screens. https://superdesign.dev/blog/ai-dashboard-ui-design
- "The Purple Gradient Problem": a persona and divergence prompting, banned lists, and "clean and modern is not a direction". https://dev.to/james_anderson_h/the-purple-gradient-problem-why-ai-ui-all-looks-alike-and-how-to-fix-it-3j65
- "Why Every AI-Built Website Looks the Same (Blame Tailwind's Indigo-500)". https://dev.to/alanwest/why-every-ai-built-website-looks-the-same-blame-tailwinds-indigo-500-3h2p

Most sources are practitioner opinion, not controlled studies. Prevalence figures quoted in the catalog, such as "roughly a quarter ship shadcn untouched", come from one source's sample and are approximate.
